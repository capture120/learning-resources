description: guideline for LLMs to better generate novel ideas
---
[From what I experience] even with current sota-super-smart models, brainstorming about an actually new problem consists for them to bring and try to combine existing bits from the literature, and not to do the real research process.

No "let's think about a minimal case", no "the fundamental properties to keep are", no "if we represent X as a Y then", etc.

Just bringing tons of existing concepts and trying to pipe one in the other.
