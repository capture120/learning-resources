- understanding agency is what matters
- understanding talent in *many* fields reach the same conclusion
- reducing activation energy, rather than relying on will is a valid strategy
- understanding priorities, motives, etc through self-reflection
- ^ focusing your actions in high imapct, high leverage areas
- and more.
