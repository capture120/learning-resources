procrastination is failure caused in many ways
non-exhausting:
- not knowing your goals
- not knowing your passions
- not being skilled at X skill
- not learning your skill in a fun way
- bottoms up learning. layer is too low. doing bottom up in an un-interesting way
- not having enough entertaining, rewarding feedback loops
- not acting enough. boring, passive learning

we can fix this by
- introspecting and knowing your goals
- doing stuff you enjoy
- get baseline skill to where learning X skill is enjoyable
- learn in a way that is "fun" to you. whatever that means
- top up learning. if bottom up is boring you, try moving one layer higher. do actionable work that is interesting. learn while building
- structure your activity to have fun rewards. why building following interests is better. rewards can be social, intrinsic, etc. use whatever means you can
- act more. do more. dont always be passive while learning. passive learning good for relaxation, but can get very boring. acting is effort, but rewarding. an investment
