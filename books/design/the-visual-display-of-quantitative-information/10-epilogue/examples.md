# Epilogue: Designs for the Display of Information — Examples

The Epilogue (p. 191) is a single page of text with no graphics, case studies, or examples. It contains only Tufte's closing statement of philosophy: design is choice, principles generate and guide options without being binding, and the designer's task is the revelation of the complex.

It implicitly refers back to the entire body of examples in the preceding nine chapters (Minard's map of Napoleon's march, Playfair's charts, the redesigned scatterplots and histograms, the chartjunk gallery, and so on), but discusses none of them individually.
